 Firstly, i have made the oscillating circuit with 2 transistors and i added an LED just for fun, for indication.Then i made 2 SR latches, each one correspond to termistor and water detector. 
 Then i made AND gate to feed those both signals. And i connected a speeker to oscilator to it to be like a siren. 
 And i works like this: in normal state oscilator is off, then if water or fire release SR latch, AND gate feeds it to the oscilator and unlocks it, making it a siren.
